What they know about you — the exhaustive list (§1.3)

  1. Your start — the safehouse is public, always.
  2. Your district at the end of Days 3 & 6.
  3. Your Day-4 move class — or "no movement" if you Held.
  4. Every drop completion: site + day, announced by you.
  5. Nothing else. Not your route, not your SP, not your Holds. Probes and stakeouts are invisible during the case.

At the paper table a stakeout question ("did your day end on X?") also leaks one watched node — a known paper artifact, logged on the dispute sheet. Don't build strategy on it; the online game is silent.

Drop discipline — the leash

Drop 1 collected — day  →  Drop 2 collected — day (only after drop 1 — standing on drop 2 early does nothing)  →  Gate — day

ESCAPE = end any day ON the gate with both drops in order. Ending on the gate with drops incomplete is legal and announces nothing (§9.5) — but the drop events convert your itinerary into public fact: every detour you buy with spare SP is ambiguity you paid for, and every day you linger is a day the calendar works for them.

Paranoia notes (legality check before you commit)

  • Afford it? Cost ≤ SP left. A 3rd Hold is not legal — plan the SP ledger, not just the route.
  • Adjacent? One edge only — check the map line between From and To. No pass-through, no shortcuts.
  • Worth announcing? Drop events, D3/D6 district, D4 class: your only public words. Everything else stays on this sheet.
  • Arm yourself: "Stranded" at 0 SP is a rule, not a failure — say it and take the free Hold (§9.13).
  • Disputes, confusions, "feels wrong": one line in Notes — verbatim quotes survive transcription best.
