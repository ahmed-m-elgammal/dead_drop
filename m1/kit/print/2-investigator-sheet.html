Endgame arithmetic

1 stakeout = 3 probes of budget. With 3 IP you either learn one yes/no about tomorrow with capture upside — or up to three pins about today with none. Time pressure reverses information-first only when the calendar, not the belief, is your enemy: a stakeout armed on Day 7 for Day 8 on the gate can steal EXPIRY into CAPTURE. Your IP is exhausted? Waiting wins only if Day 8 arrives first.
