DEAD DROP — DAY MAT · one day = these 7 steps, always in order, none skipped

ruleset 1.0.0-alpha.1 · SP 12 · IP 10 · reveals D3·D6 district, D4 transit · disputes → dispute sheet, 3-min cap
1FUGITIVE MOVES (secret)

Exactly one action: Move along one edge (Foot 1 SP · Tram 2 · Metro 3) or Hold (stay, 1 SP, max 2/case). At 0 SP: forced Hold, free — say "Stranded" (§9.13). Write it on your route sheet. Announce nothing yet.

fugitive only · ⏱ 2:00 timed by opponent · timeout = auto-Hold (free if 0 SP), log it
2STAKEOUT CHECK

Only if a stakeout was armed yesterday. The armed node is compared to today's end position: equal → CAPTURE, case over (steps 3–7 don't happen). Miss → consumed silently, play continues.

Investigator: "Stakeout check — did your day end on [node]?" · Fugitive answers truthfully: yes/no.
no clock · no armed stakeout → skip to 3
3REVEALS

Day 3 & 6: Fugitive announces the district of their end position.
Day 4: Fugitive announces the class of today's move — or "no movement" if they Held (§9.7).

fugitive announces · both record on Case Record sheet
4DROP CHECK

If end position = the pending drop site → public event: "Drop N completed at [site], day [d]". Fires after a Move or a Hold. Standing on drop 2 while drop 1 is pending: nothing fires (in order).

fugitive announces · record on Case Record sheet
5ESCAPE CHECK

Both drops collected and end position = gate → ESCAPE, Fugitive wins, case over. The gate is ordinary ground until then (§9.5) — ending there early announces nothing.

checked every day · fugitive announces only if escaping
6INVESTIGATOR ACTS

In private, on your own sheet: ≤2 Probes (1 IP each — "is your end position today X?") and/or ≤1 Stakeout (3 IP — captures tomorrow if they end tomorrow on your node; never arm on Day 8). Or wait. Unspent IP carries over.

investigator only, silent · ⏱ 2:00 · timeout = auto-wait, log it
7CLOCK ADVANCE

Day 8 ending here → verdict EXPIRY, Investigator wins. Otherwise day += 1, probe counters reset, back to step 1.

both note the new day on the Case Record sheet

Special situations — the four you will actually meet

  • Stranded (§9.13): 0 SP at the start of a day → the day resolves as a free forced Hold; Holds-used does not increase; the log says "Stranded"; if it's Day 4 the transit reveal reports "no movement". No penalty, no capture risk from the rule itself.
  • Stakeout on the node they're standing on (§9.6): legal. It captures only if they are still there at the end of tomorrow — Holding into an armed node is a capture; walking away escapes it.
  • Hold across Day 4 (§9.7): legal; the transit reveal says "no movement" — public. That's the information price of a Hold.
  • Day 8 rules: arming a stakeout is rejected (§9.3 — armed on Day 7, it still resolves on Day 8). A Day-8 probe is legal but cannot change the verdict. No escape by end of Day 8 = EXPIRY, Investigator wins.

Verdicts

ESCAPEgate + both drops, in order → Fugitive
CAPTUREstakeout hit at step 2 → Investigator
EXPIRYDay 8 ends, no escape → Investigator
FORFEIT / VOIDonline only — paper uses auto-Hold / auto-wait

No draws exist. When the verdict lands, both sheets go on the table: full logs reveal at case end (§8.6) — that's how the record gets transcribed.
