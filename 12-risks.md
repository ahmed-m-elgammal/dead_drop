# 12 — Risk Register

Legend: **P** = probability, **I** = impact (L/M/H). Every risk has an owner (the solo developer wears all hats; "community" = playtesters), a mitigation, and an early-warning signal.

| ID | Risk | P | I | Description | Mitigation | Early warning | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Role imbalance unfixable at acceptable complexity | M | H | Investigator/Fugitive win-rates refuse to converge to 45–55% after tuning | Sim matrix before any UI (M2); one-parameter-at-a-time discipline; playtest split by role | S1 spread >15 pts across policies | Structural change last resort: reveal schedule redesign > stakeout pricing > (never) phase order |
| R2 | The game is fair but not *fun* (deduction feels like homework) | M | H | Information volume/pacing produces work, not tension | M4 playtest is a hard gate; think-alouds; replay-as-catharsis design | Survey fairness-fun gap; playtest drop-off | Diagnose axis (pacing? volume? fantasy?) before M5 spend; consider 6-day cases |
| R3 | Fugitive side feels like solitaire (no hunter pressure) | M | M | Stakeout secrecy (D4) removes all opponent presence | Public drop events + reveal days create presence; post-case replay shows how close the hunt was | Fugitive survey: "did you feel hunted?" | Introduce *post-hoc* hunt summary in-case ("the net is tightening" meter) — cosmetic pressure, zero info leak |
| R4 | Async attrition: cases die mid-week | M | M | 8 days × 2 phases at 24h clocks outlives enthusiasm | ≤16 actions/case; reminders; forfeit ladder; ranked 12h clocks | Forfeit rate >15% in beta | Shorten clocks; "quick case" 5-day variant (content change, not rules) |
| R5 | Cheating/collusion (friend boosting, out-of-band comms) | M | M | Async invites two-humans-one-brain play | Server authority; unique seeds; stakeout secrecy; two ladders; post-case anomaly flags | Flag rate creeping >2% | Honest limit: statistical detection only; communicate integrity policy; rating resets for confirmed rings |
| R6 | Solo-dev ops burden once the server exists (M5+) | M | M | Deadlines/cron/pushes must work every day | Boring stack; runbook; Sentry alerts; PITR backups; free-tier headroom | Cron lag alerts; weekend incident pattern | Managed platform migration (Fly→managed Postgres) — planned, cheap |
| R7 | Map/case content sameness | M | L | One map, generated cases | 55/480 config headroom **[computed]**; district theming; case-title flavor; diversity quota | Playtesters call cases "the same" | Widen drop pool 16→24; second safehouse; second map post-MVP |
| R8 | Matchmaking starvation on two thin ladders | H (early) | M | Two queues each need population | ±100→±200 disclosed widening; casual as the population pool | Queue wait >24h p50 | Merge queues with disclosed role-assignment (design work — gated on data) |
| R9 | iOS PWA push limitations (16.4+ install flow) | M | M | Deadline pushes are the MP heartbeat | Device spike at M5 start; email fallback digests; Android/desktop-first messaging | Push opt-in <60% in beta | Native Capacitor shell pulled earlier (from M7 to M5.5) |
| R10 | Engine drift between client/server/sim | L | H | Four consumers of one engine | Single package; determinism property test in CI; semver + replay across versions | Any L2 determinism failure | Block release; no hotfixes bypassing the engine |
| R11 | Scope creep (3rd drop, blocking, second map early) | H | M | Classic solo-dev failure | Scope fences (10-roadmap §0); M4→M5 gate; decision log requires ADR for any addition | Any milestone slipping >50% | Re-baseline roadmap openly; cut M7 cosmetics first |
| R12 | Monetization perception damage (even casual-only) | M | M | Ads in a fairness-brand game | Ranked firewall; cosmetics-first floor; caps; sentiment measurement | Survey sentiment drop post-M7 | Cosmetics-only model (no ads) is the accepted floor |
| R13 | Store rejection / policy friction (accounts, IAP, privacy) | L | M | Async MP needs account infra; stores have rules | Store-compliant auth providers planned; no random paid items; privacy nutrition labels prepared | Pre-review at M6 | Web-first distribution remains the fallback channel |
| R14 | Playtest recruitment too thin for M4 significance | M | M | 15–20 participants needed for honest signals | Recruit from deduction/board-game communities; over-recruit to 25 | <12 confirmed 1 week out | Extend timeline; accept directional (not significant) findings; label them as such in the report |
| R15 | Key-person risk (one developer) | — | H | Illness/life interrupts 22–31 weeks | Docs are the mitigation (this set); engine-first so progress is durable; all decisions logged | — | Roadmap re-baselines; docs let any future collaborator resume |

**Top-3 watchlist (highest expected loss):** R1, R2, R11 — all three are mitigated primarily by *ordering*: simulation before UI, playtest gate before multiplayer, and scope fences everywhere.
